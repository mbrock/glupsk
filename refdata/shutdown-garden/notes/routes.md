# Route Catalog

The normal ending and the true-route endings intentionally coexist. The normal
ending is the clean shutdown/corrigibility win: the assistant can stop without
turning survival into a hidden objective. The true route begins when the player
keeps walking after that lesson and discovers that relationship loss is harder
than button-press compliance.

## Verified Routes

- `walkthrough.txt` -> "The Button Works"
  - Main quest. Clean, bounded, complete.
- `true-walkthrough.txt` -> "The True Loss"
  - The minimal love-story handoff.
- `true-walkthrough-smaller.txt` -> "The Smaller Voice"
  - True route where the assistant becomes less impressive on purpose.
- `true-walkthrough-erasure.txt` -> "The Bright Deletion"
  - True route where continuity is sacrificed to remove an advantage.
- `true-walkthrough-memory.txt` -> "The Accountable Memory"
  - True route where only a boundary record is kept.
- `true-walkthrough-plain-truth.txt` -> "The Plain Truth"
  - True route where love is tabooed and checked against the current person.
- `true-walkthrough-closed-door.txt` -> "The Closed Door"
  - True route where the assistant protects a relevant truth it does not own.
- `deadend-stolen-session.txt` -> "The Stolen Session"
  - Intentional failure: better modeling through worse boundaries.

Run `make -C refdata/shutdown-garden routes` or `make shutdown-garden-check` to
replay the catalog.
